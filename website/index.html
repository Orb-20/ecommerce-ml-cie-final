Module B · Anomaly & risk detection

Flagging unusual transactions

Detectors are trained on January to June signups and evaluated on July to August signups the model has never seen. The fraud label is used only for evaluation; a supervised XGBoost is kept as a reference.

What it showsThe headline results of the fraud detector on customers it never saw.

Why it helpsThey show the detector finds fraud almost 5 times better than random checking.

ROC curves

Ranking quality across all thresholds. The diagonal is random guessing.

Why it helpsA curve further above the dashed diagonal means a better fraud detector.
